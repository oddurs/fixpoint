these are subsets of the latin modern fonts (version 2.005), by bogusław jackowski
and janusz m. nowacki, based on donald e. knuth's computer modern. they are cut down
to latin characters, converted to woff2, and renamed "FP LM ..." as the gust font
license requests for derived works. the originals and their license are at
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern/ ; the license is in
GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt alongside this file.
